Inside the atelier

Four acts
of patience.

  1. 01

    The sketch

    Every commission begins as a pencil line — proportion first, ornament last, nothing that cannot be defended.

  2. 02

    The cast

    Recycled 18k gold is poured, rolled and drawn by hand until the metal finds its intended weight.

  3. 03

    The setting

    Stones are seated under 10× magnification — each claw cut, lifted and closed around its own stone.

  4. 04

    The polish

    Days of hand-finishing give the surface its depth. If it doesn't glow in candlelight, it starts again.
